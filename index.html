Tuesday, Wednesday & Thursday

Institutional weekly range expansion typically peaks mid-week (Tuesday through Thursday). Monday often serves as a range-building day after the weekend, while Friday afternoon sees position squaring ahead of the weekend close.

WHEN NOT TO TRADE (DANGER ZONES)

Avoid entering new trades during daily market rollover (4:00 AM – 6:00 AM Cambodia Time) when spreads widen sharply, and wait 15–30 minutes after high-impact US news (CPI, NFP, FOMC at 7:30 PM / 8:30 PM UTC+7) for the liquidity sweep to finish before entering.
